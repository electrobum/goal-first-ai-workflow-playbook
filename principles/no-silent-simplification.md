# No Silent Simplification

Workflow can become leaner.

Obligation scope may not become thinner.

This is the central anti-drift rule of the playbook. A rich requirement cannot disappear just because:

- revisiting it feels expensive;
- a thin placeholder is easier to produce;
- the system wants a faster coverage number;
- a short summary is more convenient than a real closure surface.

If something changes, write the change explicitly:

- implement it now;
- remap it into a richer surface;
- replace it with a better rule;
- or retire it with scope and reason.
