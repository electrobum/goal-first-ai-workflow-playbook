# File-Backed Continuity

Continuity should live in files, not in vibes.

At minimum, a recoverable long-haul workflow should leave:

- a compact machine-readable truth surface;
- a human-readable exact continuation note;
- a short audit note;
- a method-learning note;
- a validator or hard-gate state.

These files should be operational truth, not decorative summaries.

If a fresh thread can read them and resume without reloading the whole world, the continuity surface is doing its job.
